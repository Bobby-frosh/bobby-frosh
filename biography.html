BOBBY FROSH

The Story

Bobby Frosh is a Nigerian Afrobeats artist, songwriter, entrepreneur, record-label owner, and creative from Nigeria. Known for his energetic sound, Naija-inspired vibes, ambition, and independent mindset, Bobby Frosh is part of a new generation of young African creatives building their careers from the ground up.

EARLY BEGINNINGS

Where It Started

Bobby Frosh began using the name “Bobby Frosh” at the age of four. What started as a name at a very young age eventually developed into an artistic identity, brand, and vision that would become a major part of his journey.

Over the years, Bobby Frosh has continued to develop himself creatively, exploring music, technology, entrepreneurship, barbering, branding, and other areas of the creative industry.

MUSIC

The Sound

Bobby Frosh's music is rooted in Afrobeats and Nigerian street vibes, combining energetic rhythms, catchy melodies, relatable experiences, and his own unique personality.

His musical influences include artists such as Seyi Vibez and Asake, while he continues to develop his own sound and identity.

He has released songs including “Grace” and “Azonto Vibez,” while also working on larger projects and collaborations.

One of his major projects is the EP “GBEDU IS THERAPY”, featuring:

02 Go Hard

His music represents his experiences, ambition, energy, and love for Afrobeats.

GOD'S TIME ENT RECORDS

Building a Label

Bobby Frosh is also the founder and owner of God's Time Ent Records, an independent record label created as part of his bigger vision within the music industry.

Through God's Time Ent Records, Bobby Frosh aims to create opportunities for upcoming artists and help develop new talent.

The label focuses on signing artists, distributing music, supporting releases, and building artists' careers.

This makes Bobby Frosh not only an artist but also a young executive working to understand and participate in different sides of the music business.

His vision for God's Time Ent Records is to grow it into a respected independent music company capable of discovering talent, developing artists, releasing quality music, and creating opportunities within the Nigerian and African music scene.

GOD'S TIME MUSIC DISTRIBUTION

Beyond Recording

As part of his entrepreneurial activities in music, Bobby Frosh has also worked on God's Time Music Distribution, providing independent artists with opportunities to distribute their music to digital streaming platforms.

His interest in music distribution comes from his desire to understand the industry beyond simply making music. He wants to learn how music is released, marketed, distributed, and monetized while helping other upcoming artists navigate the process.

THE JOURNEY

Built Through Determination

Bobby Frosh's journey has been built through determination and learning.

He has experienced the challenges that come with being an independent young artist, including limited recording resources, difficult recording environments, limited promotional budgets, and the challenge of getting music heard by a wider audience.

Instead of allowing these challenges to stop him, he continues to learn, record, release, promote, experiment, and improve.

His story is one of starting with what you have and continuously building from there.

ENTREPRENEURSHIP

More Than Music

Bobby Frosh is not limiting himself to music alone.

He is also developing himself as an entrepreneur and has ventured into the grooming industry through Bobby Cutz & Blendz Barbing Salon.

His different ventures reflect his belief that success can come from developing multiple skills and creating opportunities rather than waiting for someone else to provide them.

VISION

Building a Legacy

Bobby Frosh's vision extends beyond becoming a recording artist.

He wants to establish himself as a recognized Afrobeats artist, music executive, entrepreneur, and brand.

His long-term goals include taking his music beyond Nigeria, performing for large audiences, collaborating with established artists, growing God's Time Ent Records, discovering and supporting upcoming talent, and building businesses that can stand on their own.

For Bobby Frosh, the goal is not simply to become famous.

It is to build a legacy.

THE FUTURE

The Journey Has Just Begun

Bobby Frosh's story is still being written.

From creating the Bobby Frosh name at just four years old to developing himself as an artist, entrepreneur, barber, and record-label owner, his journey demonstrates a willingness to learn, take risks, and keep moving forward.

With music at the center of his creative journey and God's Time Ent Records as part of his bigger industry vision, Bobby Frosh is working toward becoming a force within the Nigerian and African entertainment space.
